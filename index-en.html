Have You Faced These Failures with Generic Agencies?

Marketing healthcare requires clinical trust and ethical precision, which is why generic agency tactics consistently fall flat.

"Ads Are Active, But Zero Real Patients Show Up"

You receive dozens of price-shopping DMs asking "How much for a consultation?" then vanishing. The root cause: broadcast targeting that attracts coupon hunters instead of patients seeking clinical excellence.

"Marketers Who Don't Understand Medical Procedures"

Agencies confusing dental implants with basic fillings, or making misleading promotional promises that violate Ministry of Health guidelines and harm the clinic’s prestige.

"Massive Patient Leakage at the Reception Desk"

No matter how optimized the campaign is, if your front desk team isn't trained on patient psychology and prompt WhatsApp follow-ups, up to 80% of ad spend is wasted.
